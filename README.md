Most of my work is signal processing and machine learning on detector waveforms: finding and identifying small, real signals in noisy detectors, and checking confirming my models trained on simulation apply well to real data.

- **Now:** probabilistic pulse classification on raw LZ waveforms (PyTorch, NERSC GPUs)
- **Before:** denoising autoencoders for the NEWS-G experiment at Queen's University
